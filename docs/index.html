Grid workload characterization

What 404,176 jobs on a real computing grid look like

An analysis of the GWA-T-4 trace from AuverGrid, a production grid of five clusters (475 CPUs) in the Auvergne region of France, used mainly for biomedical and high-energy-physics research. The trace covers every job submitted during 2006.
